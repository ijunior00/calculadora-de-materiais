General Information

  • The main goal of this tool is to provide a better support for blade repair planning of material and tool estimate.
  • To estimate its quantity, it is necessary the damage information collected during the blade inspection. The user must follow the tabs sequence of required data.
  • Important note: this tool covers materials and tools for general blade shell laminate repairs. Damages requiring repairs for other blades components (e.g. LPS cable repair) should have a consult from blades technical support, or engineering.

This tool was developed by Brazil Blades Service Team

Repair Steps Input

Set the quantity of each repair step and blade region info.

Hover the i icon beside each repair step to read notes for that step. Each step quantity drives consumable, chemical and tool calculations downstream — underestimating a step understates its materials.
